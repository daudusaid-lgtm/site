What's included

Everything covered under Amazon FBA Prep.

Barcode application on every unit, verified against your Amazon listing before it leaves the line.

02

Polybagging & bundling

Suffocation-warning compliant poly bags, multi-packs, and set bundling to Amazon spec.

Inbound inspection & QC

How it works

From your PO to your shelf.

01

Receive

Your inbound shipment is scanned in against the PO at our dock.

02

Inspect

Cartons are opened, quantities verified, and damaged units flagged before prep starts.

03

Prep

FNSKU labels, polybags, and bundles are applied to Amazon's current spec.

04

Ship to FC

Prepped units are palletized or cartonized and routed to the correct Amazon fulfillment center.
