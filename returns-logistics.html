What's included

Everything covered under Returns Logistics.

01

Inspection & grading

Every returned unit is inspected and graded (sellable, refurbishable, damaged) against your rules.

02

Repackaging & rework

Sellable units get repackaged and returned to available inventory instead of sitting idle.

03

Restock or disposition

You decide the rule set — restock, liquidate, donate, or destroy — and we execute it consistently.

04

Return-to-vendor

Defective units are batched and routed back to your supplier when that's the better economic call.

How it works

From your PO to your shelf.

01

Return arrives

Inbound return is received and logged against the original order where possible.

02

Grade

Unit is inspected and assigned a condition grade using your criteria.

03

Decide

Grade determines the path: restock, rework, RTV, or disposition.

04

Execute

Unit is repackaged and restocked, or routed to its next destination, with a report back to you.
